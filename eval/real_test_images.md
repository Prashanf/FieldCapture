# Real Phone Camera Test Images (`eval/test_set/real_captures/`)

All **30** real-world evaluation photographs were captured directly by the phone camera during hands-on field testing (`Feild capture-camera-captures.zip`) and stored under `eval/test_set/real_captures/` (`4000×2252` JPEG). No downloaded stock/EPA images or synthetic frames are used in the primary benchmark.

- **Nameplates & Product Labels (`13` frames: `REAL-NP-001` – `REAL-NP-013`):** Usha Air Fryer, Ant Esports Scorpion MK20 Keyboard, Logitech M90 Mouse, Nawab Parker label, Dell WK118 Wireless Keyboard, Portronics Power Plate 10 (low-contrast embossed), Samsung Travel Adapter `EP-TA20IBE`, Samsung Refrigerator BEE energy plate — including **bright vs. dim/motion-blurry retake pairs** (`REAL-NP-009`..`013`).
- **Damage & Structural Scenes (`13` frames: `REAL-DMG-001` – `REAL-DMG-013`):** Peeling/blistered paint, wall corner mold/soot staining, window sill peeling, open ceiling drywall holes, hairline wall cracks, and under-sink cabinet staining across bright and dim lighting.
- **Note-Context Scenes (`4` frames: `REAL-NOTE-001` – `REAL-NOTE-004`):** Floor tile check, under-sink P-trap check, upper wall baseline check, and dim under-sink clamp inspection.
